# Typography Formula Reference

Use this compact index to classify visual layout in screenshots and videos.

## Use

For each key frame, choose 1-3 formulas and write how they affect implementation: focal point, grid, whitespace, density, hierarchy, material style, and motion.

## Formula Index

- Full-screen: one dominant image/type field; full but not clogged.
- Faded: low saturation, transparency, soft contrast, memory/poetry.
- Circular: central focus plus orbital flow.
- Floral: plant/flower direction guides text and visual rhythm.
- Module: grid blocks, repeated modules, hierarchy through size.
- Symmetry: axis-led calm balance.
- Asymmetry: off-center weight balanced by density/distance.
- Centered: strong central focus, breathable edges.
- Line: lines divide, guide, emphasize, or support.
- Border: frame content to gather attention.
- White space: emptiness strengthens focus.
- Column: columns and gutters organize reading.
- Curved: arcs guide visual flow.
- Retro: historical symbols with modern order.
- Minimalist: remove nonessential elements; precision matters.
- Horizontal: wide calm rhythm.
- Vertical: stacked scroll/poster rhythm.
- Misplaced: deliberate offset within invisible order.
- Wraparound: text/elements wrap around a subject.
- Collage: cutouts, stickers, photos, paper fragments.
- Text: typography itself becomes the visual.
- Handwriting: handwritten marks add intimacy.
- Ceramic: glossy rounded material, glaze, highlight.
- Glass: blur, transparency, edge light, layered depth.
- Perspective: vanishing direction and scale create depth.
- Dynamic: tilt, blur, repetition, speed cues.
- Blurred: secondary blur creates depth or memory.
- Tape: pasted label/tape material, torn edge, shadow.
- Textile: weave, fold, softness, fabric shadow.
- Color block: large planes structure hierarchy.
- Slice: cut image/type into aligned slices.
- Triangle: triangular hierarchy for stability and direction.
- Overlapping: front/middle/back depth through layers.
- Shadow: shadow as structure, material, or focus.
- Graffiti: rebellious marks, scribbles, spray, strong focus.
- Eye element: gaze direction guides attention.
- Hand-drawn: loose marks, arrows, circles at structural points.
- Gradient: directed color flow with stable content.
- Ink spread: ink bloom, feathered edge, wash, grain.
- Water ripple: wave/ripple layers, usually 3-5 levels.
- Black-white: black sets force, white sets breath.
- Organic: natural curves and irregular flow.
- Up-and-down: top/bottom dominance and zone spacing.
- Left-and-right: one side logic/detail, other side openness/image.
- Torn: torn edges at boundaries, stable center.
- Diagonal: 15-25 degrees mild motion, 30-45 strong tension.
- Three-dimensional: perspective, light, foreground/middle/background.
- Pixel: grid units, blocks, digital nostalgia.
- High-saturation: strong color impact with stable focal structure.
- Hand element: gesture direction guides attention.
- Grainy: noise/sand/paper texture gives warmth and depth.
- Sticky notes: note blocks, slight rotation, layered shadows.
- Checkerboard: repeated grid contrast.
- Golden ratio: 1:1.618 divisions and golden focal points.
