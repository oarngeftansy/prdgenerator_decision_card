# Planning Audit Rubric

Score 0–100 across:

- Scene and event coverage: 20
- Evidence alignment: 20
- Causal and state accuracy: 20
- Domain rule accuracy: 15
- Uncertainty discipline: 10
- Terminology consistency: 5
- Boundary and failure states: 5
- Implementation acceptance criteria: 5

Severity: blocker, high, medium, low. Each finding includes document location, evidence reference, reason, and remediation.
